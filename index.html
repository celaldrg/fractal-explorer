    How to explore

    • Drag to pan the picture.
    • Wheel or pinch zooms around the pointer; double-click or double-tap zooms in.
    • Shift-drag draws a zoom box; Back undoes zoom steps.
    • Keys: arrows pan, + and − zoom, 0 resets, J toggles Julia mode.
